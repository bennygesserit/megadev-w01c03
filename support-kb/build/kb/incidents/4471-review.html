Shared month-first parser

The shared parser reads dates month first even when a tenant writes day first. For example, harbour.dental report R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July. A tenant can have this parser problem through the app or email even if it runs booking-v3; having that form does not turn all its reports into hint reports.

Track this separately: parser issue #2.

Booking-v3 hint contradicts tenant date order

The booking-v3 date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, while its form reads input in the tenant’s day-first order. This affects form submissions for alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour. Booking-v2 prints DD/MM/YYYY. Copper.studio form report R-41230 and alder.physio form report R-41241 are examples of the separate hint path, irrespective of their intake tags or browser locales. Track this separately: form hint issue #1.

Correct the hint to match tenant parsing, and verify the form and shared-parser paths independently. A parser fix alone does not correct misleading form instructions.
